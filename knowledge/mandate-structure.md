## Notes on reading this table

- **"Author" means the contents page names them.** It is not a claim about who drafted
  which sentence, and it is not a claim of endorsement by their employer.
- **A person can appear more than once.** An author who also sits on the board of a
  contributing organisation has two distinct edges in the data, not one doubled one.
- **Chapter 30 is followed by an epilogue ("ONWARD!")**, whose author is not a chapter
  author and is therefore not in this table.
